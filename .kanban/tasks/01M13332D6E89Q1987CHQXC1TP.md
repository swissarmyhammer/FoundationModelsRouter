## What

`SessionOutbox` and `SessionMailbox` are public actors only because the `RoutedSession` protocol names five of their nested types (Sources/FoundationModelsRouter/Session/RoutedSession.swift:274, 278, 285, 292, 296, 308, 314). Hoist the five types to the top level, then demote the two actors.

- Move to top-level public types, with new names that stand alone: `SessionOutbox.ItemID` (suggest `PromptID`), `SessionOutbox.PromptQueueMutationResult`, `SessionOutbox.QueueDepth` (suggest `PromptQueueDepth`), `SessionMailbox.ElicitationAnswerDelivery`, and `SessionMailbox.ElicitationCompletionDelivery`.
- Update the `RoutedSession` protocol, `RoutedSessionActor`, and every other reference.
- Demote the `SessionOutbox` actor (Sources/FoundationModelsRouter/Session/SessionOutbox.swift:24) and the `SessionMailbox` actor (Sources/FoundationModelsRouter/Hosting/SessionMailbox.swift:15) to `internal`.
- Do not keep public deprecated aliases; this package is pre-1.0 and the break is accepted.
- Update the DocC pages that name the old nested paths.

This is a mechanical rename across many files; the two demotion tasks it depends on must land first so the moves do not collide.
