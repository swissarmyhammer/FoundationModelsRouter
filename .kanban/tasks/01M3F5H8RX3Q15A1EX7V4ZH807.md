## What

Found during ^7w145zc. Under the parallel stress of that task (12 processes x 4 repetitions of the whole bundle and 8 processes x 60 repetitions of `AnswerCancellationTests`, load average 59 to 84 on 18 cores), these tests failed at their 5-second `BoundedWait` bound or at their 60-second `.timeLimit`, in 3 of 3 rounds:

- AnswerCancellationEntryPointTests "a respond cancelled while its message waits behind another submission never reaches the model, and records nothing": `BoundedWait.conditionReached("the second message waiting in the outbox")`.
- MessageQueueTests "a respond whose message waits is withdrawn by cancel(message:) ...": `BoundedWait.conditionReached("the respond message waiting in the outbox")`.
- RespondRunPlaneDrainTests "streamEvents(to:) emits runSettled for a run that settles before the stream ends": `BoundedWait.conditionReached("\(count) runs tracked on the session")`.
- GenerationStallDiagnosticTests "a streaming answer reports the stall against the fragments it counted": "Time limit was exceeded: 60.000 seconds" (11 to 12 times each round).
- ExecutorPassBoundaryTests "stream: a consumer slower than generation keeps no pass open": `passDurations.allSatisfy { $0 < Self.slowConsumerPause }`.
- AnswerCancellationCompactionTests "cancelling a caller-driven compact() that waits behind a running answer ...": `actor.pendingCompactions.count == 1`.
- Round B of ^7w145zc (16 processes x 3 repetitions of the whole bundle) also had HumanWaitGateTests and GenerationStallDiagnosticTests `SignalNeverArrived`, and RunToCompletionRunnerTests `AnswerNeverDelivered`.

^7w145zc showed for one test that such a failure was the wall-clock bound, not a lost event: the event came later. The fix there was an `AwaitedEvent` that the producer signals, so only the `.timeLimit` of the suite ends the wait.
